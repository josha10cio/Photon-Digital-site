- Badge: AI-Powered • Digital Twin Technology
- Title: Digital Twin Systems
- Intro: Digital twins extend the same simulation pipeline used for training into live testing and monitoring. They help teams validate models and track performance in realistic conditions.
- What a Twin Provides:
  - Real time simulation and dashboards
  - Safe testing for changes and updates

- Data Loop: Synthetic data trains the model. The twin tests the model. Results guide the next dataset generation.
